# Beneath the surface — September 2026

## Central concept

A business has a visible surface and a much richer structure underneath. The opening is a visitor-controlled architectural cutaway: public impression, underlying intelligence, and execution. The original Draper Norwood logo remains the identifying anchor throughout.

The experience then changes around the visitor's challenge. Positioning, digital experiences, AI and practical systems, and creative growth each have a distinct authored explanation, working model, and order of real evidence. An optional public website read brings the visitor's own material into the experience and produces something they can keep or bring to a conversation.

## Why this belongs to Draper Norwood

The company connects diagnosis with execution across disciplines. The site makes that relationship visible, then demonstrates the judgment involved in choosing a useful next step. The archive case remains important evidence, while the broader strategy, digital, systems, and commercial work leads the experience.

## What changed from the first concept

- Restored the actual original lockup and monogram; removed the substituted identity.
- Replaced the archival worktable with a continuous three-layer model, controlled by pointer, touch, button, or keyboard range input.
- Added four challenge-led perspectives and four different interactive demonstrations. The site explains which models are illustrative or curated.
- Added a real, bounded public HTML reader with downloadable observations and explicit inquiry prefill. No simulated AI answers or invented diagnostics.
- Reordered truthful case evidence by the visitor's selected challenge. Lost Archives remains available and appears last on the homepage.
- Rebuilt the visual system around near-black green, warm paper, restrained perspective-specific accents, self-hosted typography, and the existing identity.
- Retained four case routes, evidence assets, validated inquiry delivery, analytics, metadata, sitemap, robots, and original anchor compatibility.

## Creative-director refinement

The complete first implementation was reviewed in-browser. The highest-leverage corrections were to give mobile its own cutaway scale and space, strengthen body reading sizes, remove a competing oversized section number on mobile, simplify the landing line breaks, and move visitors directly into their selected perspective with accessible focus handling. The final pass concentrated on clarity and interaction continuity, without starting another concept.

## What was rejected

An archive-dominated identity was too narrow for the company's intended work. A freeform AI chatbot would have made the experience depend on generic generated advice and obscured what had actually been inspected. Decorative particles, generic three-dimensional objects, audio, scroll hijacking, and forced introductory sequences did not earn their place. The model uses the brand and the company's actual way of working as its material.

## Evidence and limits

The case claims and historical source assets retain the provenance documented in `RELEASE-NOTES.md`. No clients, testimonials, awards, revenue, or outcomes were added. Models explain a method; they are not presented as client results. The public reader separates page observations from a tentative next-step hypothesis and does not claim access to internal information.

The inquiry provider is mocked in automated tests; no real email was sent during this release. Production delivery requires the existing verified Resend domain and `RESEND_API_KEY`. The public reader and inquiry have bounded per-instance rate limits, not distributed abuse controls. The original production site is not replaced by this preview branch.
