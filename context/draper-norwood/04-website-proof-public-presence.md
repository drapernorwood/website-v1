## Website Purpose

The Draper Norwood website should quickly establish:

- what Draper Norwood is
- who it helps
- what problem it solves
- why the work matters
- what kinds of work it does
- proof that it can execute
- a clear path to start a conversation

The website does not need to explain everything.

It needs to create trust, clarity, and momentum.

## Current Website Positioning

Draper Norwood is a strategy, web, and AI execution studio for companies that need sharper positioning, stronger digital assets, and measurable business improvement.

## Homepage Job

The homepage should answer:

- What is this?
- Is it credible?
- Is it relevant to me?
- What problem does it solve?
- What kind of work does it do?
- Is there proof?
- What should I do next?

## Homepage Sections

Recommended homepage structure:

### 1. Hero

Purpose:

Make the company clear and compelling immediately.

Possible headline direction:

“Make your business clearer — and harder to ignore.”

Supporting idea:

Draper Norwood helps companies clarify their value, strengthen their digital presence, and present themselves with greater precision, credibility, and force.

Use “force” carefully. Alternatives include:

- weight
- clarity
- authority
- confidence
- conviction
- presence
- impact

### 2. The Gap

Purpose:

Explain the central problem.

Core idea:

A business can have real value, strong proof, and serious capability, but still be underperceived because the market only meets the form it is given.

Useful line:

“The market does not meet your work through your private understanding. It meets the form you give it.”

### 3. What We Do

Purpose:

Show the service categories without turning the site into a bloated service menu.

Categories:

- Positioning & Strategy
- Web & Digital Presence
- AI & Modern Systems
- Creative & Growth Assets

### 4. Selected Work / Proof

Purpose:

Show credibility and execution without over-selling.

Should feel curated, not noisy.

### 5. Founder Perspective

Purpose:

Show James’ point of view, judgment, taste, and strategic lens.

This can be short. It does not need to become a personal biography.

### 6. Contact / Start The Conversation

Purpose:

Give a clear, low-friction next step for qualified opportunities.

Preferred language:

“Start the conversation.”

## Proof Strategy

Proof should be presented with restraint and intelligence.

The goal is not to shout metrics.

The goal is to show that Draper Norwood understands how to turn strategy, media, technology, and execution into real-world outcomes.

## Proof Presentation Rules

Do:

- design proof as polished assets
- explain why the result matters
- connect proof to business capability
- keep case studies concise
- show strategic thinking and execution
- use metrics where accurate
- label uncertainty clearly when needed

Do not:

- dump screenshots without context
- make proof feel like bragging
- imply client details that are not public
- leak sensitive internal communication
- overstate causality
- make every proof item look like a social media win

## LinkedIn Direction

LinkedIn should support authority, not performative posting.

Good content territories:

- why Draper Norwood exists
- the gap between actual value and public form
- lessons from rebuilding the website
- proof packaging
- selected work
- AI as leverage, not substitute
- founder-led company clarity
- services + products future
- strategic observations from real work

Avoid:

- fake CTAs
- “I’m excited to announce”
- agency clichés
- forced vulnerability
- generic clarity posts
- engagement bait
- overexplaining every decision

## Launch / Public Live Post Direction

The public live announcement should not sound like a typical launch post.

It should feel:

- human
- thoughtful
- grounded
- strategic
- precise
- slightly poetic
- not salesy

Key idea to preserve:

“The market does not meet your work through your private understanding. It meets the form you give it.”

Do not say:

- looking for clients
- book a call now
- excited to announce
- big things coming
- after months of hard work
- we help brands unlock growth

## Website Decision Rule

When editing the site, prioritize:

- clarity
- premium restraint
- proof
- trust
- business relevance
- public confidence
- clear next step

Do not redesign for novelty.

Refine what already works.
